Good questions

Everything you're wondering

Do I own my website?

Absolutely. Once your project is paid for, the site is yours — code, content, and domain. You're never locked in, and you can take it anywhere. The care plan is entirely optional.

What do you need from me to start?

Not much. Your logo if you have one, a few photos, and a sense of what makes your business special. If you don't have those ready, that's exactly what the free consultation is for.

How long does a website take?

Most one-page Storefront sites launch in about a week. A full Main Street site usually takes two to three weeks, and Landmark projects vary with scope. You'll get a clear timeline before we begin.

Can you update my existing website instead?

Often, yes — but for businesses on old, slow template sites, a fresh custom build is usually faster, cheaper to maintain, and far better looking. I'll give you my honest recommendation either way.

What if I need changes after launch?

Small edits are included with the care plan, and larger changes are always available at a fair rate. Either way you get a real person who knows your site — not a support ticket queue.

Do you only work with businesses in your town?

Main Street is a feeling, not a zip code. I work with local businesses anywhere — most of the process happens over a friendly call and email, so your town can be my town too.

Still deciding? Let's talk it through

No hard sell, no obligation. Tell me about your business and I'll recommend the right fit and an honest price.
