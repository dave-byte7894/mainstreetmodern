Our story

Built by hand, made for Main Street

With care, with craft, and with the people who live there in mind. Mainstreet Modern exists for one reason: local businesses deserve websites as good as anything the big chains have — and a whole lot warmer.

The short version

A designer who builds for local businesses

I started Mainstreet Modern because I kept seeing the same thing: incredible local businesses stuck with websites that didn't come close to doing them justice.

Slow, clunky, template sites that looked like everyone else's, while the big chains pulled customers right past the local shops with slick, fast, beautiful websites. That never sat right with me.

So I decided to level the field — combining modern design and the newest tools to build genuinely world-class sites, priced so a Main Street business can actually afford one. That's the whole idea.

— Davis

A website should feel like walking into a well-kept shop.

Too many websites feel like a maze. Mine are built to feel like a handshake — clear, friendly, and pointing your customer exactly where to go. Because the goal was never just a pretty page. It's more calls, more bookings, more people choosing you.
